# HeapLine user requirements

What the people who would use or stock HeapLine need, written from their side. The product requirements that meet these needs are in the PRS (`03-requirements.md`) and the links are in the traceability matrix (`03b-traceability.md`). Method: STANDARDS section 22.

*Table 1. User requirements.*

| ID | Category | Title | Need | Priority | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UR-001 | Deployment and assembly | First search line fast | In the first minutes after a slide, the people on site can fetch the kit and start searching before formal responders arrive | High | Waste pickers and cooperatives (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-002 | Performance | Find a buried person | Searchers can locate a buried colleague at the depth people are typically buried, from the surface | High | Waste pickers and cooperatives; responders (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-003 | Performance | Work on loose waste | Searchers can move over loose, voided debris without sinking or falling through | High | Waste pickers and cooperatives (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-004 | Performance | Clear heavy cover | Sheeting, timber or heavy debris lying over a mark can be moved when hands alone cannot | High | Waste pickers and cooperatives (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-005 | Performance | Move a casualty | A casualty can be moved over debris to a pickup point by the people on site | High | Waste pickers; neighbours (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-006 | Usability | A clear point to stop | Every searcher knows the stop signals and the safe zone before stepping onto the debris | High | Neighbours; responders (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-007 | Environment and durability | Ready after months in a shed | The kit works on the day after months stored in heat, damp and dust | High | Site operators and municipal staff (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-008 | Logistics | Carried by few people | The kit reaches the slide carried by four people or fewer, with nothing too heavy for one person | High | Waste pickers and cooperatives (PRB constraints) |
| UR-009 | Cost | Affordable to stock | A site or a cooperative can afford to stock and inspect the kit | High | Site operators and municipal staff (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-010 | Safety | Does not add danger | The equipment cannot make a bad situation worse: no rope overload, no drum run-back, nothing that pulls on a person | High | All users (PRB safety note); decisions 2, 3 and 6 of HPL-DDR-001 |
| UR-011 | Maintenance | Repairable locally | The kit can be inspected monthly and repaired with common steel, timber, rope and tools | Medium | Site operators and municipal staff (PRB constraints) |
| UR-012 | Usability | Hand over an organised search | Responders arrive to a search with a lookout, marked burial points and a clear handover | Medium | Fire, police and Red Cross responders (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-013 | Regulatory | Not mistaken for certified equipment | The kit is published and described as an open engineering reference, not certified rescue equipment | Medium | Site operators and municipal staff; responders (PRB safety note) |
| UR-014 | Performance | Work in the dark and rain | Searchers can see and be seen at night and in rain, without power at the scene | Medium | Waste pickers and cooperatives (PRB operating environment) |
| UR-015 | Logistics | A shared capstan arrives in time | Where neighbouring sites share one capstan set, it arrives while the search is still under way | Medium | Site operators; decisions 23C and 3A (HPL-DDR-003, HPL-DDR-004) |

Categories: deployment and assembly, performance, usability, safety, environment and durability, logistics, maintenance, cost, regulatory. Priorities: High, Medium, Low.
